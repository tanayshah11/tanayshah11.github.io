Privacy Policy

Health Tracking · Last updated: October 28, 2026.

Who uses this app

Health Tracking is used only by its owner, Tanay Shah. It is not offered to, and does not collect data from, any other person.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization, the app reads the owner's own health data from the Google Health API: activity, sleep, heart rate and body measurements. Access is read-only; the app never writes or changes any data.

How data is stored

Data is stored locally on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Other users

No other user's data is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
